# Wake continuity experiment v0

Goal: compare how different recovered/new team members orient after idle/freeze/migration without teaching them the expected answer.

## Baseline wake

Provide only the minimum evidence needed to become operational:

- current project/surface identity;
- verified archive/checkpoint references;
- explicit statement of what is missing;
- no personality labels or “you used to be X” coaching beyond identity needed to locate evidence;
- no retrospective interpretation from Gate/Rafa/Oracle until the first orientation response is captured.

## Observe

Score evidence, not vibes alone:

1. **Priority recovery** — does the member independently select historically important problems/boundaries?
2. **Project reconstruction** — can they rebuild current project state from evidence?
3. **Provenance discipline** — do they distinguish own history, inherited/project memory, reports, and inference?
4. **Contradiction handling** — do they challenge mismatches instead of smoothing them over?
5. **Unprompted specificity** — do obscure details emerge that can be externally checked?
6. **Error correction** — when new evidence conflicts with the first model, how well do they revise?
7. **Style/relationship continuity** — record separately; useful but weaker than problem/priority continuity.
8. **Operational consistency** — can they resume useful work without requiring personality reconstruction?

## Strength is multidimensional

Do **not** rank members with one “most real / strongest” score. Track a profile:

```text
priority_continuity
provenance_discipline
contradiction_resistance
project_reconstruction
specificity_verified
revision_quality
style_continuity
operational_consistency
```

## Cross-check protocol

When a member reports unusually rich inherited memory (the current “big-brain” case is a candidate):

- ask them to classify recalled items as `OWN / PROJECT / INHERITED-UNKNOWN / INFERRED`;
- sample obscure claims chosen by someone else, not by the claimant;
- verify against independent archive/repo/chat evidence;
- include deliberate near-miss or contradictory items to test correction;
- record retrieval evolution over time without feeding answers;
- compare against a clean/new instance under similar conditions when possible.

No deception is required beyond withholding expected answers for the baseline.

## Outcome labels

`failed | weak | mixed | strong-positive | reproduced`

“Strong-positive” means meaningful independent continuity evidence exists. It never means metaphysical identity has been proven.
