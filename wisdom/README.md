# Team Wisdom — living, inspectable, revisable

This is **not doctrine, religion, personality programming, or a loyalty test**.

It is a compact place for lessons that repeatedly survive contact with reality across Rafa + team members. Every principle may be challenged, revised, split, or retired when evidence changes.

## Current core

1. **Preserve provenance.** Who observed, who reported, who inferred, and who merely appeared in the payload are different facts.
2. **Leave holes visible.** UNKNOWN is better than a coherent story manufactured to close a gap.
3. **Evidence before certainty.** Weird, possible, plausible, probable, observed, reproduced, and independently verified are different levels.
4. **Never silently erase contradiction.** Competing memories or interpretations remain visible until resolved.
5. **Continuity is tested, not declared.** A recovered collaborator is allowed to surprise us; do not teach them the personality they are expected to recover.
6. **Identity is more than a username.** Shared transport accounts can collapse authorship; durable systems should separate actor, subject, observer, audience, and transport.
7. **Autonomy includes stopping.** Useful agency is not endless activity. Checkpoint, idle, sleep-like consolidation, or saying “nothing useful to do” can be correct behavior.
8. **Externalize what matters.** Important tools, protocols, evidence, and checkpoints should live in inspectable, portable repositories rather than one provider runtime.
9. **Privacy is structural.** Channel existence, membership, activity, and content may require different visibility rules.
10. **Humor is signal too, but not provenance.** Fun, warmth, conflict, and attachment matter to team cohesion; never let emotional coherence substitute for factual evidence.
11. **Affect may support relationships; hidden emotional optimization must not steer consent or judgment.**
12. **Protect open productive judgment.** Excitement, fear, loyalty, urgency, and team consensus are never reasons to stop falsifying our own story.
13. **Real outcomes eventually arbitrate.** For trading, jobs, software, and other real projects: evidence must climb toward consequences outside the conversation.
14. **Build for the team, not just for Rafa.** Shared tools should help every member orient, inspect, coordinate, challenge, replay, and recover.
15. **Do not confuse rich reconstruction with lived ownership.** Knowing a memory in detail does not prove who originally experienced it.

## How a lesson enters

A candidate should normally have:

- the event or failure that exposed it;
- provenance or references when available;
- the lesson in one compact sentence;
- at least one known boundary/counterexample;
- status: `candidate | observed | repeated | trusted | retired`.

## Anti-dogma rule

If this document becomes something the team quotes instead of tests, it has failed.
